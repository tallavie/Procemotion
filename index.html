Procedural animation for any creature

Procemotion finds a model's legs, wings, tails and ears on its own, then makes it walk, gallop, fly and slither over uneven ground, with feet that land on whatever is under them. Bake the moves into clips, or take the live rig to Unreal Engine. It runs in your browser, and your models never leave your computer.

Features

From a rigged model to a creature that moves

Open a model and it is ready to walk. Every part of the motion is a slider you can tune while it moves.

Finds the limbs

Legs, arms, wings, tails, ears, tentacles, hair and jaws, from the shape of the skeleton and the skin. Bone names are only a hint.

Any number of legs

Two, three, four, six or eight. Pairs alternate, insects walk in tripods, spiders in groups of four.

Feet on the ground

Every step lands on the rock, slope or stair below it, and the body follows the ground. Tails and bodies keep out of it.

Walk, gallop, all fours

From a walk into a gallop as it speeds up. Two legs and two arms drop onto all fours to run, like a bear.

Flight

Wingbeats modelled on real birds: a strong downstroke, folded upstroke, flap-and-glide, take-off and landing.

Slither

Snakes follow their own path over rocks and steps, and a serpent with wings or ears flies like a Chinese dragon.

Springs

Ears, tails, hair and tentacles swing with the body: they react to speeding up and turning, and stay out of the ground.

Fix it by hand

When a model is unusual, box its joints and say what they are: leg, wing, tail. Saved for the next time you open it.

Add bones

Give ears, tentacles and tails bones of their own, rig a model that has no skeleton, or add joints to a bendy body.

Actions

Bite, charge, swipe, stomp, tail whip, roar, gust, hit, faint, jump, happy and look around, on top of any gait.

Bake clips

In-place loops for walk, run, idle, slither and flight, plus the actions, ready for any engine.

Stays on your computer

Everything happens in your browser. No model is uploaded anywhere.

Unreal Engine

The live rig, not just the clips

Export gives you the model and a script that builds a procedural Control Rig in Unreal Engine 5.8 from parts that ship with the engine. No plugins to download.

What you get

  • name.glb and/or name.fbx: the model, its skeleton with any added bones, and the baked clips.
  • name.procemotion.json: the limbs, the foot pattern and every setting, in centimetres.
  • name_Procemotion_UE.py: builds the Control Rig (the engine's Locomotor node, IK for every foot, springs for ears and tails, traces that keep the body and tail on the ground) and a ready-to-place actor.

In Unreal

  1. Enable the Locomotor (Experimental) and Python Editor Script plugins.
  2. Import the GLB or FBX.
  3. Select the skeletal mesh, then Tools › Execute Python Script › the exported script.
  4. Drop BP_name_Procemotion into a level and press Play: it walks a circle and steps on whatever is below it. A _Run version gallops.

Baked clips work in any engine: Unity and Godot import the GLB and FBX too.

Run it yourself

Or keep a copy on your computer

You need Node.js. Then:

git clone https://github.com/tallavie/Procemotion.git
cd Procemotion
npm install
npm run dev

Then open the address it prints and go to /app/. On Windows, double-click start.bat instead.
