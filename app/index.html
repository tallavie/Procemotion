Needed for walking creatures: UE's Locomotor starts from the reference pose, and some models are bound lying down. It can shift blended areas slightly in the model's own clips, so it is off for creatures without legs.

You get name.glb and/or name.fbx (mesh, skeleton with any added bones, clips; the FBX has the textures inside and one take per clip), name.procemotion.json and name_Procemotion_UE.py, which builds the Control Rig in UE 5.8 from either file. Shape keys (morph targets) are not exported.
